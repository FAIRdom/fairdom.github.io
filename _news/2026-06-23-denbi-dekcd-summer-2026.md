---
title: "Joint de.NBI & de.KCD Summer School 2026 - featuring FAIRDOM-SEEK"
---

28. September - 2. October 2026, Gießen, Germany

Join **de.NBI** and **de.KCD** for an intensive five-day Summer School at the intersection of life sciences, cloud computing, and research data management. Researchers at all career stages and highly motivated students will explore state-of-the-art methods in genomics, metagenomics, and pangenomics, from reproducible workflows on the de.NBI Cloud to sustainable data sharing with **FAIR** principles. The Summer School will be a mixture of key-notes, expert lectures and hands-on tutorials and offers, beyond the technical skills, a unique opportunity to connect with community experts as well as other researchers.

Day 5 teaches Research Data Management, featuring [FAIRDOM SEEK](/fairdomseek) from eductors Ulrike Wittig and Wolfgang Müller (HITS, de.NBI SysBio).

For details, please visit the [event page on the de.NBI website](https://www.denbi.de/training-courses-2026/2108-joint-de-nbi-de-kcd-summer-school-2026-cloud-enabled-meta-pan-genomics).
